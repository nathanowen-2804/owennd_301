Parks in Harrisonburg

Explore local parks for walking, sports, picnics, and outdoor recreation.

Hillandale Park

Address: 801 Hillandale Avenue

Hillandale Park offers walking trails, playgrounds, picnic shelters, and mountain biking trails.

Activities: Hiking, biking, basketball, volleyball, and picnicking.

Hillandale Park Information

Purcell Park

Address: 41 Monument Avenue

Purcell Park features a walking trail, sports courts, picnic shelters, and the Kids Castle playground.

Activities: Walking, tennis, pickleball, baseball, and playground activities.

Purcell Park Information

Westover Park

Address: 305 South Dogwood Drive

Westover Park features a disc golf course, skate park, playground, swimming pool, and recreation center.

Activities: Disc golf, skateboarding, swimming, and picnicking.

Westover Park Information

Ralph Sampson Park

Address: 431 East Washington Street

Ralph Sampson Park has basketball courts, futsal courts, a playground, walking paths, and a seasonal sprayground.

Activities: Basketball, futsal, walking, and playground activities.
