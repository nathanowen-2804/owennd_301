Accessible Outdoor Activities

Explore recreational activities around Harrisonburg with accessible features. Click a location to learn more.

A Dream Come True Playground

Address: 1050 Neff Avenue

An inclusive playground featuring accessible equipment, family comfort stations, and a wheelchair-accessible swing.

The Liberty Swing requires advance arrangements and training to use.

A Dream Come True Playground Information
Edith J. Carrier Arboretum

Location: James Madison University

The Plecker Pond Loop is a wheelchair-accessible trail surrounding the pond, with opportunities to enjoy gardens and wildlife.

Other arboretum trails may have more difficult terrain.

Edith J. Carrier Arboretum Information
Hillandale Park

Address: 801 Hillandale Avenue

Picnic shelters 1, 3, 6, 7, 10, and 12 are wheelchair-accessible. The park also has accessible comfort stations.

Accessibility of individual walking trails may vary.

Hillandale Park Accessibility Information
Northend Greenway

Location: Northern Harrisonburg

A shared-use walking and biking path with a hard surface and generally gentle slopes.

Some sections include street connections. Check the route before visiting to ensure it meets your accessibility needs.
